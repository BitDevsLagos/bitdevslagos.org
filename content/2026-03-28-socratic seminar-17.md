# Topics

### Arkade and the Ark Protocol

MC: Joshua Arokhai

Joshua opened with an introduction to Ark Labs and the Ark protocol: why the project matters and what it aims to achieve on Bitcoin, especially when it comes to Bitcoin programmability.

Topics covered: what Ark is and how to enter the Ark, batch outputs, VTXOs and atomic VTXO refresh, Ark transactions, unilateral exits, constraints, Arkade Assets and Arkade Scripts.

### Bitcoin and Nostr Video Calls

MC: Chuks Agbakuru

Chuks walked the room through a video conferencing product he is building, which uses Bitcoin and Nostr for real time charges between callers on the platform. The session ended with a product demo.
